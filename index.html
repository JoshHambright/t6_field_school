    Your T6, mapped

    Everything here refers to the stock Rebel T6 (EOS 1300D) with the EF-S 18–55mm kit lens. Menu names can vary slightly by firmware, so keep the printed manual handy for edge cases.

    Where the controls live

    Main dial

    Behind the shutter button. Changes the primary setting for the mode you are in (see table).

    Shutter button

    Half-press to focus and meter; the readout stays alive a few seconds after you let go. Full press to shoot.

    ISO button

    On top, just behind the main dial. Press, then turn the dial. ISO steps in full stops: 100, 200, 400 … 6400, plus H (12800) once it's enabled in the custom functions.

    Av± button

    Beside the LCD, top right. Hold it and turn the dial: exposure compensation in P/Tv/Av, aperture in M.

    Q button

    Opens the Quick Control screen: metering, AF mode, drive, WB, Picture Style and image quality in one place.

    ✱ (AE lock)

    Top right on the back. Locks exposure so you can meter one spot and recompose. An asterisk appears in the viewfinder.

    AF point button

    Next to ✱. Press it, then use the dial or cross keys to choose one of the 9 AF points. SET jumps back to the center point.

    Cross keys

    ▲ White balance · ◀ Drive (single, continuous, 10 s / 2 s timer) · ▶ AF operation (One Shot, AI Focus, AI Servo) · ▼ Picture Style.

    Lens switches

    AF/MF for manual focus. STABILIZER ON/OFF: on when handheld, off on a tripod.

    Specs that change how you shoot

    Sensor18 MP APS-C, 1.6× crop. Your 18–55 frames like a 29–88 on full frame. Hand-holding rule: 1/(focal × 1.6).
    Shutter30 s to 1/4000, plus Bulb (M mode, turn the dial past 30"). Flash sync up to 1/200.
    ISO100–6400, H = 12800. Above 1600 noise becomes visible; 3200 is fine for small prints and the web.
    Autofocus9 points, the center one is cross-type and the most reliable in low light.
    MeteringEvaluative, Partial (about 10% at center), Center-weighted. No spot meter: Partial is your precision tool.
    DriveAbout 3 frames per second continuous.
    ViewfinderAbout 95% coverage: the photo shows a little more around the edges than you saw.
    Exposure comp.±5 stops in ⅓-stop steps (the viewfinder scale shows ±2).
